# Android Mockup 002 — Compact Schedule

A denser schedule-first Android mockup for users who want quick scanning and fewer decorative elements.

## Design intent

- More utilitarian Material feel: tabs, list rows, badges, and a persistent bottom action.
- Keeps the next dose visible while emphasizing the full day schedule.
- Includes a reminder-status panel reachable from the tab strip.
- Treats T-blocker as optional and explicitly mentions hiding it for estrogen-only plans.

## Interaction included

- Click "Take" to change the next-dose action to a completed state.
- Click the "Reminders" tab to reveal the local reminders status panel.

## Best fit

Good if phase 1 should optimize for information density and fast schedule checks over emotional warmth.
