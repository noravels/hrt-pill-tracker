# Android Mockup 003 — Routine Timeline

A routine-oriented concept that frames doses as a calm daily timeline rather than a task list.

## Design intent

- Warm neutral palette for a less clinical but still adult and trustworthy feel.
- Shows completed, next, and optional future dose slots in chronological order.
- Strong privacy framing for local-only phase 1 use.
- Explicitly supports estrogen-only routines by making the T-blocker slot optional and removable.

## Interaction included

- Tap the check button in the hero card to mark the next dose as done.
- Tap "Open reminder details" to expand a bottom sheet with local reminder status.

## Best fit

Good if the app should emphasize rhythm and continuity, especially for users who benefit from seeing where they are in the day.
